What stands out

Five details that matter in day-to-day use

These are practical buying points—not just a list of marketing phrases. Match each one to the type and volume of wire you actually process.

01 / CHANNELS

Six round channels + one flat slot

Multiple entry sizes make it easier to select a channel for different cable diameters instead of relying on a single opening. The stated working range is 0.06–0.98 inches (1.5–25 mm).

02 / MOTOR

60W geared motor

The product is motor-driven, reducing the need to power the stripping action by hand. VEVOR describes the gear motor as delivering performance comparable to a larger 180W motor; that equivalence is a manufacturer claim, not an independently verified test result.

03 / VISIBILITY

Clear acrylic viewing panel

The transparent cover gives a view of the internal feed and stripping area. That can help when checking alignment or observing how a new wire type moves through the machine.

04 / KIT

Gloves and user manual included

The product listing says a pair of gloves and an instruction manual are included. Treat the gloves as an accessory, not a substitute for safe handling, eye protection or proper machine procedures.

05 / BUILD

Cast iron body, bench-tool footprint

Listed dimensions are 18.9 × 6.7 × 12.6 inches and weight is 34.2 lb. The solid build suits a dedicated work area better than frequent carry-around use.

06 / ADJUSTMENT

Marked sizes and dual adjustment knobs

Channel markings and two upper knobs are designed to help set the clamping plate for different wire sizes. Expect to make careful adjustments and test on a short sample before processing a full batch.

Balanced buying advice

Where the VEVOR makes sense—and where it may not

Reasons to shortlist it

  • You regularly handle more than one wire diameter and want several marked channel options.
  • You prefer a motorized stripping action instead of supplying a separate drill.
  • Your cable sizes fall within the stated 1.5–25 mm range.
  • You value a visible work area and a substantial cast iron body for a fixed bench setup.
  • You have enough repeat work for setup time to pay off over repeated manual stripping.

Reasons to compare alternatives first

  • You need to move the machine frequently: 34.2 lb is not lightweight.
  • You process cable larger than 25 mm: this model's stated upper limit will not cover it.
  • You want a published feet-per-minute throughput figure: one is not specified in the listing.
  • You expect every wire type to work without adjustment: blade height and feed alignment may need tuning.
  • You only strip a few short pieces occasionally: a hand tool or drill-powered unit may be more economical.

Frequently asked questions

What wire sizes does the VEVOR BXJDD60 support?

The VEVOR US listing specifies 0.06–0.98 inches (1.5–25 mm) and seven channels: six round openings and one flat slot. Check the wire's actual outside diameter and construction before feeding it through.

Does it run on standard US power?

The listed US version is specified as AC 110V / 60Hz. Confirm the voltage and frequency shown for the exact variant selected on the product page before purchase.

Is it fully automatic?

It uses a motorized drive, but it is not a set-and-forget sorting system. The user still needs to choose a suitable channel, set the blade or clamping height, align the wire and supervise the operation.

Will it work with every cable type in the size range?

No machine should be assumed to handle every insulation, strand or cable construction identically. The stated diameter is a useful first filter, but cable shape, insulation and condition can affect feeding and stripping. Test a short, de-energized sample first.

Is it suitable for a portable setup?

It weighs 34.2 lb and measures 18.9 × 6.7 × 12.6 inches according to the listing. It is better treated as a bench tool than a lightweight portable hand tool.

Does the coupon guarantee a $167.92 checkout total?

No. $167.92 is the coupon price quoted in the campaign information supplied for this page. The live product page showed a different pre-coupon price at the time of checking. Confirm the coupon, eligibility, shipping and tax in the VEVOR cart.

Can it make copper recycling profitable?

A stripper can reduce manual insulation removal work, but it cannot guarantee a profit. Results depend on the amount and type of wire, local scrap prices, labor, electricity, machine setup and other operating costs.

What safety precautions should I take?

Only process disconnected, de-energized wire. Follow the user manual, keep hands clear of blades and moving feed parts, secure the machine on a stable work surface, and use appropriate eye and hand protection. Never feed live electrical cable into the machine.
