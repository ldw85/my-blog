## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Mac mini good for everyday use?**
Absolutely. For browsing, email, documents, video calls, and streaming, Mac mini is overkill in the best way — everything feels instant. It's quieter and takes up less space than any laptop with similar performance.

**Can Mac mini replace a laptop?**
With a monitor, keyboard, and mouse, yes. Mac mini gives you desktop performance without a built-in screen. Many people pair it with a portable monitor for a flexible setup.

**Does Mac mini come with a keyboard and mouse?**
No — those are sold separately. Apple Magic Keyboard and Magic Mouse are popular choices. You can also use any USB-C or Bluetooth keyboard/mouse.

**How many monitors can Mac mini support?**
M4 supports up to 3 displays (two 6K Thunderbolt + one 5K/8K HDMI). M2 Pro supports up to 3 displays with higher bandwidth for smoother multi-monitor workflows.

**Does Mac mini have Wi-Fi?**
Yes — both M4 and M2 Pro have Wi-Fi 6E and Bluetooth 5.3 built in.

**Can you upgrade memory later?**
No — unified memory is soldered to the chip. Choose your memory configuration at purchase. This is why many users opt for 24GB or 32GB upfront if budget allows.

**Is Mac mini good for AI and local models?**
Yes. M4's Neural Engine (38 TOPS) handles local AI inference well. You can run models via Ollama, GPT4All, or native Apple Intelligence features. The unified memory architecture means faster model loading compared to traditional PCs.

**What's included in the box?**
Mac mini, power cord, and that's it. No keyboard, mouse, or monitor. Plan to budget $100-300 extra for peripherals if you don't already have them.
