## What Actually Happened

### Week 1: Almost Gave Up

I'm going to be completely honest — the first week I almost sent these back.

The heel cup felt *too* tight at first. Like my heel was being squeezed. And the arch support? Way more aggressive than I was used to. By the end of day 1, I was thinking "what did I just waste $35 on?"

But I stuck with it. Here's what I learned: **these insoles need a break-in period**. I started wearing them for just 2-3 hours a day, then gradually increased.

### Week 2: Things Started Clicking

By day 10 or so, something changed. The foam started giving a little where my foot pressed down, but the heel cup still held firm. It's like the insoles were actually learning my foot shape.

That weekend, we did our usual 3-mile park walk. Normally my heels would be throbbing by dinner. That night? Barely any discomfort. Not saying I was pain-free, but the sharp "walking on needles" feeling was noticeably softer.

### Week 3-4: Pretty Dang Good

Now I've been using these for about a month. Here's my honest assessment:

- **Standing all day at work**: Way better. I can actually make it to 5 PM without that familiar heel ache creeping in.
- **Walking**: Solid support, no sliding around.
- **Running**: Tried two 5K runs. Not great. Too bulky for running, my foot moved around too much. These are walking shoes only.
- **My wife tried them too**: She's 130 lbs. Said they felt "fine" but didn't notice much difference. So if you're lighter, the effect might be less dramatic.

## FAQ

**How long do these last?**
About 6-12 months depending on use. Heavy users (250+ lbs) might replace sooner. Mine look fine after one month.

**Will these fix my plantar fasciitis?**
I'm not a doctor, but my podiatriend did say these types of insoles help manage symptoms. They didn't "cure" me, but they made a real difference in daily pain levels.

**Can I use these for running?**
No. Get dedicated running insoles. These are too bulky and don't have the lateral support running needs.

**Do they work for wide feet?**
Yes — these are actually designed for wider feet. If you have narrow feet, you might need to trim them more.
