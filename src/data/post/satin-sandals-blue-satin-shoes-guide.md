## Care Tips for Satin Shoes

Satin requires a bit of special care to keep it looking its best:

- **Storage**: Keep satin shoes in their dust bags or shoe boxes to prevent dust and scratches
- **Cleaning**: Use a soft, damp cloth for spot cleaning
- **Protection**: Apply a fabric protector spray specifically designed for satin
- **Weather**: Avoid wearing satin shoes in rain or wet conditions
- **Stains**: For stubborn stains, consider taking them to a professional shoe cleaner
